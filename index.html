A real moment from a Lagos home

"Mummy, can we do God time first?"

Last Sunday after church, my friend Tolu sat down with her 5-year-old daughter and asked her what she remembered from children's church. Zara stared at her tablet. "Mummy, can I have my iPad?"

That was the moment Tolu realised something quietly devastating. Her daughter could sing every Cocomelon song. Could mimic three YouTubers. Knew which buttons to press to skip ads on YouTube Kids. But couldn't remember a single Bible story from that morning.

Sunday school had 45 minutes a week. The screen had 5 hours a day. The numbers were never going to work, no matter how loving the parent.

So I built her something. A 56-page printable activity pack designed around a 10-minute daily rhythm called Print-Pray-Play. Two weeks in, she sent me a voice note. "Last night Zara asked me if we could do our God time before her bath instead of after, because she didn't want to be too sleepy for it." Two weeks. From iPad to God time.
